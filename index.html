Animal Spies

Networked multiplayer mobile game · C++, CUGL/SDL · 2024

Built for Cornell's advanced game development class and awarded Most Innovative at the end-of-semester showcase. The game is a 1v1 duel in two phases: first you place traps to stop your opponent from reaching the key and the exit, then you run for the exit yourself while dodging their traps and setting off your own.

I was Programming Lead for the seven programmers on the team, helping design the system architecture and leading code review. I built the trap-placement phase and its touchscreen UX. I also implemented movement, collision, and the elevation and render order logic behind the game's 3/4 perspective.

Bubblegum Bandit

Action platformer · Java, LibGDX, Box2D · 2023 · free on Steam · GitHub source

You play as a space rogue out to bring down a robot regime, flipping gravity and spitting gum to infiltrate spaceships, steal their power sources, and get out before everything blows up. Built with a team of eight for Cornell's intro game development class.

I contributed to concept and level design, and programmed the dynamic scrolling camera, the gum mechanics, doors and crushers, and the Tiled level parser. I also worked on the initial Box2D lights bring-up, collision handling, and level loading.

WebGL Infinite World

First-person exploration · TypeScript & Three.js · 2024

A procedurally generated, never-ending world. Built with two others for Cornell's Intro to Computer Graphics class. I wrote the player controls, an infinite terrain system with chunks, and the atmospheric haze shader. I also tuned the noise function driving the world generation.
